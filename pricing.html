What changes the final price?

Page count, original design, content preparation, product uploads, integrations, migrations and custom features all affect scope.

What about deposits and payment plans?

Payment amounts, stages and timing are confirmed in the written proposal. The website does not take payment or commit you to a contract.

Who owns the finished website?

Ownership, account access, editable files and any third-party licence conditions are defined in your agreement before work begins.

Can I start small and expand?

Yes. We can plan an initial scope around essential needs and discuss later phases separately.
