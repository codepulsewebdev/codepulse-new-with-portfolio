How to choose a website developer in Johannesburg

Questions to ask a Johannesburg website developer about scope, SEO, ownership, support and the customer journey.

By CodePulse Web DevelopmentReviewed 28 October 2026Planning guide
IN THIS GUIDELook beyond the opening screenAsk how the site will support your businessAsk for a clear SEO approachConfirm ownership and accessAgree on scope and responsibilitiesDiscuss life after launch

Look beyond the opening screen

Inspect a developer’s site on your phone. Try navigation, forms and project examples. Attractive design matters, but your customers also need a usable path to the next step.

Ask how the site will support your business

A good discovery conversation covers your services, ideal customers, locations and what counts as a worthwhile enquiry. Ask how those answers affect the page plan.

Ask for a clear SEO approach

Discuss search intent, page titles, useful service content, internal links and technical checks. Be cautious of guarantees about first position on Google.

Confirm ownership and access

Clarify domain registration, hosting, business accounts, content rights and editable files. Know what you receive at handover and what depends on third-party licences.

Agree on scope and responsibilities

Confirm page count, features, content delivery, feedback stages and revision rounds. Check how additional work is approved and priced.

Discuss life after launch

Ask about updates, support channels, maintenance scope and how to request improvements. The right arrangement depends on your team’s capacity and platform.

This is general planning guidance. Your final scope, costs and contractual terms depend on your project.
