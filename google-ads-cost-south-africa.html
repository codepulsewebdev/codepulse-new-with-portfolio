Google Ads costs: what should your budget include?

A practical guide to separating Google Ads media spend, management, landing pages and measurement costs.

By CodePulse Web DevelopmentReviewed 28 October 2026Planning guide
IN THIS GUIDEStart with an outcomeSeparate media spend and managementAllow for the destinationPlan measurement before launchTreat forecasts as scenariosAsk the right questions

Start with an outcome

Define the valuable action: a qualified enquiry, a booking or a sale. A click is useful only when it supports the business goal.

Separate media spend and management

Media spend pays the advertising platform. Management covers the agreed work of planning, setting up, monitoring and improving campaigns. CodePulse quotes Google Ads management after discovery.

Allow for the destination

Your landing page needs to match the advertisement and make the offer easy to understand. Design, development and content work may be separately scoped.

Plan measurement before launch

Agree which actions count as conversions and how they will be measured. Respect applicable privacy requirements and avoid counting the same enquiry multiple times.

Treat forecasts as scenarios

Auction prices and conversion rates vary. Use assumptions to test a budget, then update the plan using actual campaign and sales data.

Ask the right questions

Ask who owns the account, what reporting includes, how changes are approved and whether external tools or creative production create additional costs.

This is general planning guidance. Your final scope, costs and contractual terms depend on your project.
