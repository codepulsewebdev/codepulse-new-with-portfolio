How much does a website cost in South Africa?

Understand website pricing, what changes a quote and how to plan a realistic South African website budget.

By CodePulse Web DevelopmentReviewed 28 October 2026Planning guide
IN THIS GUIDEStart with the job your website needs to doWhat changes the price?CodePulse starting pointsSeparate the ongoing costsCompare scope before comparing totalsGet a useful estimate

Start with the job your website needs to do

A brochure website, an online store and a custom booking platform need different amounts of planning and development. Begin with your customer journey and the pages or functions required to support it.

What changes the price?

Page count, original design, content preparation, product uploads, payment integration, migrations and custom functionality all affect scope. A useful quote makes these assumptions explicit.

CodePulse starting points

Our published starting points are R1,200 for a basic 1–2 page website, R2,000 for an intermediate 3–5 page website, R3,500 for an advanced website and R4,000 for an e-commerce website. These are entry prices, not a promise that every project fits the minimum scope.

Separate the ongoing costs

Ask about hosting, domain renewal, software licences, maintenance and advertising spend. Confirm billing frequency and what happens when a subscription ends.

Compare scope before comparing totals

Check content responsibilities, revision rounds, integrations, ownership, support and handover. A written scope makes competing proposals more meaningful.

Get a useful estimate

Use our project builder to explore a starting scope, then send the brief for a tailored quote. Its estimates are indicative, and any final price must be confirmed in writing.

This is general planning guidance. Your final scope, costs and contractual terms depend on your project.
