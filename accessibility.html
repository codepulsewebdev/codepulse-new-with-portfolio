Our approach

This site is designed with responsive layouts, semantic landmarks, labelled controls, visible keyboard focus and a skip-to-content link. Accessibility is an ongoing part of design and development rather than a one-time feature.

Navigation and motion

The main navigation and interactive controls are intended to work with a keyboard. Reduced-motion preferences disable decorative transitions and reveal effects. Essential content is available without reveal animations.

Forms and demonstrations

Forms use explicit labels and browser validation. Changes to estimates and enquiry status use live announcements where appropriate. The storefront and booking interactions are clearly labelled demonstrations.

Known limits

No independent accessibility certification is claimed. External apps, sign-in interfaces and contact services are controlled by their providers. Browser and assistive technology differences can affect the experience.

Report a barrier

If something is difficult to use, email codepulsewebdev@gmail.com with the page, task and any relevant browser or assistive technology details. We can discuss an alternative way to provide information or start an enquiry.
