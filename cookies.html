What this site uses

This website does not install analytics or advertising cookies. It uses browser local storage for a privacy preference and an optional project draft. Local storage is a browser technology distinct from cookies; it can persist across visits.

Privacy preference

The key cp-privacy stores whether you have chosen to allow a saved project draft. It is written when you save your privacy choices. You can change your choice through the footer’s Privacy choices control.

Optional project draft

The key cp-project-draft stores the project brief you choose to save on this device. It can contain your entered project and contact details. It is written only after your “Save on this device” action or, while saving is enabled, when you update the builder. It is not sent to CodePulse by that storage action.

Manage or clear storage

Use “Clear saved draft” in the project builder, turn off draft storage in Privacy choices, or clear this website’s storage through your browser settings. Clearing the draft removes the saved copy; values already in an open form may remain until the page is refreshed or edited.

External services

Google Fonts receives font requests. WhatsApp, Instagram and your email provider apply their own technologies when you open those services. ChatGPT Sites hosting and access control may use essential technologies managed by the platform, separate from this website’s own code.

Future changes

No analytics or advertising consent switches are offered because those tools are not installed. If tracking is added later, the notice and consent implementation must be reviewed before activation.
